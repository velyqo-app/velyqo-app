# 🚀 Velyqo Product Roadmap

**Version:** 0.3.0  
**Last Updated:** July 2026

> **Engineer Your Future**

---

# 🌍 Vision

**Velyqo is the AI-powered Career Operating System that helps professionals discover, build and accelerate their careers through daily guidance, intelligent coaching and measurable progress.**

Rather than simply recommending careers, Velyqo becomes a trusted daily companion that helps users continuously improve their careers.

---

# 🎯 Mission

**Help professionals make one meaningful career improvement every day.**

Everything we build should support this mission.

---

# ⭐ Product Pillars

## 🏠 Career Brief

Know what matters today.

A personalised daily briefing that tells users exactly what they should focus on.

---

## 🔥 Momentum

Build consistency.

Reward progress through streaks, completed missions and positive reinforcement.

---

## 🤖 AI Coach

Receive personalised guidance.

An AI mentor that understands each user's goals, progress and current mission.

---

## 🧭 Career Journey

See where you're heading.

A visual roadmap showing career progression, milestones and salary growth.

---

## 📖 Career Journal

Remember how far you've come.

A living record of completed missions, achievements and career milestones.

# 🌍 Long-Term Vision

**Velyqo becomes the operating system professionals rely on to grow their careers throughout their working lives.**

The app should feel like having a trusted career mentor available every day.

---

# 🏛 Guiding Principles

- Every day has a purpose.
- Build momentum, not pressure.
- Explain why recommendations matter.
- AI should coach, not wait.
- Progress should always be visible.
- Build fewer features, but make them exceptional.
- Design for motivation, not information.
- Every screen should answer:
  > **"What's the next meaningful step in this person's career?"**

---

# 🌟 North Star
