# Velyqo Product Principles

## Mission

Help professionals make one meaningful career improvement every day.

---

## Vision

Become the Career Operating System professionals rely on throughout their careers.

---

# Core Philosophy

People don't need another AI app.

They need a trusted career companion.

Every feature should help users become more capable, more confident and more prepared for the careers they want.

---

# The Five Pillars

## 🎯 Career Readiness

Can I do the job today?

Measures how prepared a user is for their target career.

---

## 🔥 Career Momentum

Am I consistently moving forward?

Measures habits, consistency and daily progress.

---

## 💪 Career Confidence

Am I becoming more capable?

Measures growth through consistent learning and achievement.

---

## 📖 Career Journey

How far have I come?

Celebrates milestones and documents professional growth.

---

## 🚀 Career Potential

Where could I be if I continue at this pace?

Forecasts future career progression based on current behaviour.

---

# Product Rules

Every screen should answer one question.

"What should I do next?"

---

Every recommendation should explain why it matters.

---

Every interaction should encourage action.

---

Every completed task should feel meaningful.

---

AI should coach.

Not simply answer questions.

---

Progress should be visible.

Growth should be celebrated.

---

# Engineering Principles

Keep architecture simple.

Prefer reusable services.

Prefer shared types.

Prefer composition over duplication.

Build features that last.

---

# Definition of Premium

Premium is not visual effects.

Premium is trust.

Premium is clarity.

Premium is speed.

Premium is consistency.

Premium is thoughtful design.

Premium is making every minute inside Velyqo worthwhile.

---

# North Star

Help someone become better today than they were yesterday.
